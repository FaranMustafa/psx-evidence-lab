01 · The problem

New investors get signals without track records.

A

Screens with no history

A screen says "breakout" or "value". It doesn't say how stocks it flagged actually did over the following weeks, after charges, compared with simply owning the index.

B

Filings with no context

A dividend notice, a board meeting and a regulatory action all land in the same feed. A first-time investor can't tell which kinds of news have historically mattered, and in which direction.

C

Costs nobody sees

For short trades, commission and exchange charges quietly decide whether a trade wins or loses. New investors rarely see the move they need just to break even.

D

Tips with no accountability

WhatsApp groups and "sure shot" calls are never scored. That is how investing gets called satta, and how new investors get burned and leave.

02 · What I've already built

A point-in-time research engine for PSX.

Since July 2026 I've built a system that collects official PSX data, then tests ideas the way an honest analyst would. Each test uses only what was knowable at that moment, counts the real costs, and compares the result with the KSE-100.

It's designed to give honest answers, not flattering ones. When the evidence is missing, the system says so instead of guessing.

  • Official data collectionEnd-of-day and intraday prices, market watch, sectors, company announcements, payouts and financials. Collection is resilient to portal changes.
  • No hindsightEvery record knows when it happened and when it became known, so a test can never peek at the future.
  • Track records after costsAny rule-based idea can be replayed over five years, net of charges, against the KSE-100.
  • Announcement study5,926 filings classified and measured for what followed, relative to the market.
  • Plain-language deliveryDaily evidence cards on a market-hours schedule, a web app, and a research interface AI assistants can query.

03 · An example of what the evidence says

Not all announcements are equal.

Across 5,926 PSX announcements over five years, the type of filing mattered:

  • Dividend notices were followed by a small, short-lived rise compared with the market over the next few sessions.
  • Regulatory actions were followed by weakness that lasted weeks.
  • Results and board meetings showed no consistent edge either way.

This is exactly the kind of context a first-time investor needs next to a headline. It isn't a prediction: it's what happened before, stated plainly.

04 · What it means for Nafa

An evidence layer for Ticker Analysts and the Nafa app.

Ticker Analysts already does the hard part: screens, filings, insider moves and broker picks in one place, delivered faster than anyone else. The missing piece is proof: showing users what each signal has meant before. That proof is what this engine produces.

1 · Screen scorecards

Each of the advanced screens gets a track record: how often the stocks it flagged beat the KSE-100 over the following weeks, after charges, with the misses shown openly.

For users: they know which screens to take seriously. For Nafa: no other PSX platform shows this, and it turns casual browsers into confident, repeat investors.

Nafa already delivers filings within seconds. Next to each AI summary, add one line of history: what this type of announcement has typically meant for the stock compared with the market.

For users: they understand the news, not just read it. For Nafa: the fastest feed in Pakistan becomes the smartest one too.

Before a user confirms a trade, show the price move they need just to cover Nafa's commission and the standard PSX, NCCPL, CDC and tax charges. Then link to a short lesson on holding period.

For users: fewer losing trades that were really just fees. For Nafa: it shows off the low 0.15% commission and builds the kind of investor who stays.

05 · Why it matters beyond one app

Better-informed investors make a healthier market.

For the first-time investor

Plain answers to "has this worked before?", "what does this news usually mean?" and "what do I need just to break even?", in the app they already use.

For the market

When signals carry records, rumour and pump-style tips lose their pull. Participation grows on understanding, not speculation, which is the shift PSX and the SECP want as retail numbers climb.

For Nafa

Trust is the moat in retail broking. A platform that shows its evidence, including when an idea didn't work, earns the investors who stay for a decade.

06 · A concrete first step

A two-week pilot.

  1. Week 1Track records for two of Ticker Analysts' screens, chosen by the Nafa team, computed point-in-time over PSX history and after charges.
  2. Week 2An announcement-context prototype for the main filing types, ready to show next to the existing AI summaries.
  3. Hand-overA short write-up of method and limits, plus the data and code you need to run it yourselves, whatever the results show.
